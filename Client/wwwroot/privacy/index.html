PulsR Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026.

1. Who we are

PulsR is a transportation management application operated by AMF Carrier (“we,” “us,” or “our”) at https://tms.amfcarrier.com/. This policy explains how we handle personal information through PulsR and its connected business communications, including WhatsApp when that integration is enabled.

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or requests to delete data, contact anton@amfcarrier.com with the subject “PulsR Privacy.”

This policy covers authorized application users, drivers, and other people whose information is used in our transportation operations. Where another organization uses PulsR to manage its own workforce or business records, that organization determines the purposes of that processing. We will help direct requests concerning its records to the appropriate organization.

2. Information we collect

The information processed depends on your role, the features used, and the integrations enabled by an authorized administrator:

  • Account information: name, email address, company association, user identifiers, account status, permissions, and display preferences.
  • Driver and business contact information: names, phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers, email addresses, and customer, broker, and operational contacts.
  • Transportation records: dispatches, loads, pickup and delivery details, addresses, schedules, driver and vehicle assignments, route and fuel plans, fuel-card identifiers, and related business documents.
  • Fleet and location information: vehicle GPS coordinates, vehicle telemetry, mileage, fuel information, driver duty status, and hours-of-service information received from connected fleet systems. Vehicle location may identify the location of an assigned driver.
  • Communications: incoming and outgoing business messages, their text, attachments, captions, sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, message IDs, and delivery or read status when provided by the messaging service. Authorized staff may view these records in PulsR.
  • Integration information: connected business-account and phone-number identifiers, authorization settings, and credentials or tokens needed to operate administrator-enabled integrations.
  • Technical information: authentication and session records, browser storage, application activity, error information, and operational logs. Hosting and connected services may also process IP addresses and browser or device information when you access the application.

We receive information directly from users and contacts, from authorized company personnel, and from connected services. Please provide only the information needed for the business purpose and avoid sending unrelated sensitive personal information through messages or attachments.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users and control access; coordinate dispatch, routes, vehicles, drivers, fuel stops, and deliveries; exchange operational messages and documents; maintain business records; support users; investigate errors and security incidents; and meet applicable legal, transportation, recordkeeping, and dispute-resolution obligations.

Location and hours-of-service information support fleet visibility, operational planning, estimated arrival times, and driver scheduling. We obtain required consent or rely on another basis permitted by applicable law. This policy does not itself replace any separate notice or consent required for employee monitoring, location tracking, or optional communications.

4. WhatsApp and Meta information

When enabled, PulsR uses the WhatsApp Business Platform to send and receive messages through an authorized business number. We process the phone numbers, message content, media, business identifiers, and status information needed to provide that service. Business administrators may also authorize Meta account information and permissions needed to connect the business account.

Messages sent to our business may be stored in PulsR and made available to authorized dispatchers, administrators, or other staff handling the request. Using WhatsApp does not make those business messages inaccessible to our organization once they are received. The integration is not intended to provide access to your unrelated personal WhatsApp conversations.

We use Meta Platform information for the authorized business functions described in this policy. We do not sell it or use it for unrelated advertising. WhatsApp and Meta also process information under their own applicable privacy policies, including https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.

To request that we stop contacting you through WhatsApp, contact anton@amfcarrier.com or tell the staff member handling your conversation. Opt-out requests are handled by our team; this policy does not promise an automated keyword-based opt-out. Where ongoing operational communication is necessary, we will discuss an alternative communication channel.

5. When we share information

We share information with authorized personnel who need it for their work, and with operational contacts such as drivers, customers, brokers, shippers, or receivers when necessary to coordinate the relevant transportation service.

Depending on the integrations enabled, service providers may include:

  • Meta/WhatsApp, for business messaging and account connection.
  • Samsara, for connected fleet, vehicle, location, and driver information.
  • Google services, for maps and, where configured, business email, file storage, or cloud infrastructure.
  • Torque and other configured transportation systems, for importing or exchanging operational records.
  • Providers of hosting, storage, security, and technical support needed to operate the application.

We limit disclosures to the relevant purpose and require appropriate protection when providers process information on our behalf. A provider may also process information independently under its own terms and privacy policy. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security. We do not sell personal information.

6. Browser storage

PulsR uses browser local storage to maintain sign-in sessions and remember preferences such as display settings and filters. Connected services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Clearing or blocking browser storage may sign you out or prevent application features from working. Sign out when using a shared device.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain personal information for as long as needed for the purposes for which it was collected, including operational continuity, applicable legal recordkeeping obligations, security, and resolving disputes. Retention varies by record type; closing a user account does not necessarily remove related transportation records or business correspondence.

When information is no longer required, we delete it or de-identify it, subject to applicable legal requirements. Deletion from active systems may precede expiry from backups. Information in a connected company storage service may also be subject to that service's trash, backup, and retention settings. We will explain any applicable retention exception when responding to a deletion request.

8. Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and withdraw consent where processing depends on consent, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

To request deletion of PulsR or Meta/WhatsApp-related data:

  1. Email anton@amfcarrier.com with the subject “PulsR Data Deletion.”
  2. Provide your name, the email address or phone number associated with the records, and a brief description of what you want deleted. Use the international country code for a WhatsApp number.
  3. We may request limited additional information to verify your identity or authority. Do not send passwords, API tokens, full payment-card details, or identity documents unless we separately explain a necessary, secure verification process.
  4. We will review the request, act within the period required by applicable law, and explain the outcome, including any information we must retain and the reason.

Disconnecting an integration stops future access through that connection but does not automatically erase information already stored in PulsR. Deleting information from PulsR does not automatically delete copies held by message recipients, your employer, or independent service providers. Requests about those copies may need to be made to the respective organization.

You may raise privacy concerns with us using the contact above and, where applicable, with the relevant privacy regulator, including the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at https://www.priv.gc.ca/.

9. Security and international processing

We use authentication, access controls, and other technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Please report suspected unauthorized access to our privacy contact.

Information may be processed outside your province or country by our service providers, depending on their infrastructure and the services enabled. It may be subject to the laws and lawful access requirements of those jurisdictions. Contact us for information about the providers and processing arrangements applicable to your records.

10. Children and policy changes

PulsR is intended for business and transportation operations, not for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through PulsR, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

We will update this policy when our practices change and identify the effective date of the published version. We will provide additional notice and obtain consent where required for material changes.
